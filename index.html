Who we are

We are the student chapter of the Association of Energy Engineers at Amman Arab University. We bring together students who care about how energy is made, saved and used, and turn that interest into workshops, site visits and projects. From solar and storage to efficient buildings, we meet where classroom theory meets the energy questions Jordan faces today.

Why join

  • Learn beyond the lecturesWorkshops on energy efficiency, solar, storage and energy auditing.
  • Work on real projectsTeam challenges and site visits that put the theory to use.
  • Meet the industryTalk with engineers, mentors and companies working in energy.
  • Prepare for credentialsGet a head start on professional energy certifications.
  • Lead somethingRun an event or join a committee, and build a CV that shows it.
